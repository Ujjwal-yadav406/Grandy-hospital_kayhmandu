ABOUT GRANDE CITY HOSPITAL

Healthcare with compassion, professionalism and modern care

Grande City Hospital Pvt. Ltd. is a 50-bed private healthcare facility located at Jyoti Bhawan, Kantipath, Kathmandu. Established in 2013 as part of the Jyoti Group, the hospital provides outpatient, inpatient, emergency, diagnostic and specialized healthcare services.
